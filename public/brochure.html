Practice Profile · 07
Capability Statement

How We Support a Project

The practice was founded in 1994 and has operated as Vadalkar & Associates since 2006. It is led by Hemant S. Vadalkar, who has worked in structural design since 1989. Assignments span new design, independent review, audits, repairs, specialist analysis, and construction-stage engineering support.

Concept & Structural Design

  • Preliminary design and structural arrangement
  • Analysis, member design, detailing, and drawings
  • Approximate structural quantities

Independent Technical Review

  • Proof checking and design review
  • Structural audit and stability assessment
  • Repair and retrofit recommendations

Procurement & Site Support

  • Tender preparation and scrutiny
  • Contractor-selection recommendations
  • Periodic construction and repair visits

Analysis & Software

Advanced STAADPro and computer-aided structural analysis for buildings, industrial systems, towers, marine works, temporary works, and specialist structures.

Training & Knowledge

STAADPro seminars and technical training since 1996, supported by published papers, professional lectures, and continuing engagement with engineering institutions.
